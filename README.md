## 01 Foundational Concepts of Analytics
*   **01. Business Analytics:** Broad practice of exploring data statistically to solve business problems and guide strategy. *(HBR, 2023)*
*   **02. Data Analytics:** Technical engineering discipline focused on cleaning, modeling, and processing raw datasets. *(Evans, 2020)*
*   **03. Business Intelligence (BI):** Aggregating and reporting historical metrics via dashboards to monitor past performance. *(McKinsey, 2024)*
*   **04. Data-Driven Decision Making:** Management culture requiring verified statistical data to validate major investments. *(HBR, 2023)*
*   **05. Big Data:** Complex data fields requiring non-relational tools due to immense Volume, Velocity, and Variety. *(Investopedia, 2026)*
*   **06. Descriptive Analytics:** Diagnostic step tracking historical metrics to answer "What happened?" *(Evans, 2020)*
*   **07. Diagnostic Analytics:** In-depth data drilling and tracking used to answer "Why did it happen?" *(McKinsey, 2024)*
*   **08. Predictive Analytics:** Machine learning models evaluating trends to figure out "What is likely to happen?" *(Investopedia, 2026)*
*   **09. Prescriptive Analytics:** Advanced optimization systems automatically recommending "What should we do?" *(Evans, 2020)*
*   **10. Data Literacy:** Professional capability to read, work with, analyze, and communicate data tools accurately. *(HBR, 2023)*

## 02 Data, Measurement & Reporting
*   **11. Structured Data:** Highly organized database fields stored in clean rows and columns like SQL or Excel. *(Investopedia, 2026)*
*   **12. Unstructured Data:** Complex information lacking a set data model, such as video clips, PDFs, or native voice notes. *(McKinsey, 2024)*
*   **13. Data Warehouse:** A centralized storage archive acting as a single source of truth for analytics. *(Evans, 2020)*
*   **14. Data Mining:** Process of running computer algorithms to uncover hidden statistical correlations in vast data arrays. *(Statista, 2026)*
*   **15. Data Visualization:** The translation of raw analytics trends into intuitive graphs, heat maps, and diagrams. *(HBR, 2023)*
*   **16. Dashboard:** Live, interactive screen displaying continuous feeds of operational performance indicators. *(Google, 2025)*
*   **17. Metric:** Any measurable quantitative value used to track routine corporate operations. *(Evans, 2020)*
*   **18. Key Performance Indicator (KPI):** Critically prioritized metrics that directly measure core corporate health. *(HBR, 2023)*
*   **19. Benchmark:** External or internal baseline references used to gauge current team performance. *(Statista, 2026)*
*   **20. Segmentation:** Grouping a customer footprint into subcategories based on demographic or behavioral data traits. *(HubSpot, 2025)*

## 03 Customer & Sales KPIs
*   **21. Conversion Rate:** The percentage of platform users who take a specific commercial action out of a total target pool. *(Google, 2025)*
*   **22. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Total sales and marketing expenses divided by the number of new customers acquired. *(HubSpot, 2025)*
*   **23. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV / LTV):** Total net margin generated per consumer account over their corporate lifespan. *(Investopedia, 2026)*
*   **24. Churn Rate:** The relative percentage baseline tracking existing customer departures and canceled subscriptions. *(McKinsey, 2024)*
*   **25. Customer Retention Rate:** Mathematical inversion of churn tracking long-term consumer brand loyalty. *(HBR, 2023)*
*   **26. Net Promoter Score (NPS):** Standardized loyalty index tracking brand referral probability from a scale of -100 to +100. *(Bain, 2024)*
*   **27. Average Order Value (AOV):** Total top-line revenue divided by the absolute number of individual sales transactions. *(Google, 2025)*
*   **28. Sales Funnel:** Conceptual multi-stage mapping of user progression from Awareness to purchase. *(HubSpot, 2025)*

## 04 Marketing & Campaign Analytics
*   **29. Marketing Campaign:** Coordinated messaging deployment across various channels aiming for a unified corporate target. *(HubSpot, 2025)*
*   **30. Post-Campaign Analysis:** Retrospective deep-dive measuring performance benchmarks after a campaign ends. *(McKinsey, 2024)*
*   **31. Incremental Revenue:** Isolated net-new revenue generated exclusively as a result of an ongoing promotion. *(Evans, 2020)*
*   **32. Return on Investment (ROI):** Classic profitability test calculating financial returns against initial resource investments. *(Investopedia, 2026)*
*   **33. Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** Top-line marketing efficiency tracking gross revenue earned per currency unit spent on ads. *(Google, 2025)*
*   **34. Click-Through Rate (CTR):** The percentage tracking user clicks on hyperlinks relative to total asset impressions. *(Google, 2025)*
*   **35. Bounce Rate:** The percentage baseline evaluating web users who leave a platform after visiting only one page. *(Google, 2025)*
*   **36. A/B Testing:** Experimental practice of running dual split options to identify which version yields a superior conversion. *(HBR, 2023)*
*   **37. Lead Generation:** Pipeline tracking operations converting anonymous prospects into warm customer email leads. *(HubSpot, 2025)*
*   **38. Marketing Attribution:** Analytical logic models distributing fractional conversion credit to variable digital touchpoints. *(Google, 2025)*
